Why it is built this way

It sends the second someone signs up. No delay, because that minute is the warmest they will ever be.

1
The answer they asked for comes first. They signed up to get their picks. The button takes them straight to the right products. No code to hunt for, and no discount to train them on.
2
Goldbar Whiskey in your own words. Two lines from your homepage say who you are. Email 1 stays short on purpose. Your story gets its own email later in the flow.
3
Your products, one tap from the product page. Real products and prices from your store. Someone who has not decided yet gets a place to start.
4
The reorder reminder, set up from email one. The flow already knows when the product runs out, so the reminder lands before they buy it somewhere else. A reply line from your team makes it read like a note from a person.

Emails 2 to 5 teach: your story, the questions buyers ask, reviews, and how to get the most from what they bought. Full price, no code: the value is the product and the timing.
